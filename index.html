risk:red, orange, safe, none
os:windows, linux, macos, unknown
subnet:name or CIDR, or internet
state:established, time_wait, listen…
role:server, client, both, unknown
tag:DC, DB, Web, RDP…
collected:yes or no
seen:both or one (ends)
